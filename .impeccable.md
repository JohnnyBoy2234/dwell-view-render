## Design Context

### Users
**Primary:** Landlords — property owners managing listings, tenant applications, KYC, maintenance, payments, and viewings. They may be non-technical, juggling multiple properties, and often interacting on mobile via the Capacitor-wrapped app. Their context is task-heavy: reviewing applications, approving documents, sending payment requests, tracking maintenance.

**Secondary:** Tenants — applicants and renters completing forms, submitting documents, paying rent, and logging maintenance. They interact under time pressure and some stress (applying for a home is high-stakes).

**Tertiary:** Admins — internal ops team reviewing KYC, support messages, and document approvals.

### Brand Personality
**Trustworthy. Calm. Professional.**

The platform handles financial data, legal documents, and people's homes — the interface must never feel chaotic, flashy, or uncertain. Every screen should feel like it was built by people who take responsibility seriously.

### Emotional Goals
**Ease and clarity.** Complex rental admin — credit checks, DocuSign flows, Paystack onboarding, maintenance threads — should feel effortless. Reduce cognitive load at every turn. When a landlord opens the dashboard, they should immediately know what needs attention and what's under control.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **iOS-inspired**: The codebase already has deep iOS-style tokens (rounded corners, layered shadows, blur effects). Lean into this — it signals quality and familiarity on mobile.
- **Light + dark equally polished**: Both modes should feel intentional, not an afterthought. Light mode is clean and airy; dark mode is deep and refined, not washed out.
- **Subtle animation**: Aurora backgrounds, fade-ups, and soft floats are appropriate. Avoid flashy or distracting motion. Framer Motion transitions should feel natural, not performative.
- **Color story**: Ocean blue (`hsl(214 100% 59%)`) as primary trust signal. Green (`hsl(142 72% 44%)`) for success/completion. Earth warm tones for warmth and approachability. Avoid high-saturation decorative colors.
- **Typography**: Inter var — clean, legible, modern. Hierarchy matters more than variety.
- **References**: Apple's Settings app (clarity), Linear (professional + dark mode), Airbnb (trust + property context)
- **Anti-references**: No gamification, no aggressive CTAs, no clutter, no landing-page-style marketing within the app shell

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — every element should serve a clear purpose. If a user has to think about what something does, simplify it.
2. **Trust through consistency** — spacing, color, and component behavior should be predictable. Surprise is the enemy of trust in a financial/legal product.
3. **Mobile is primary** — the Capacitor wrapper means many landlords and tenants are on iOS/Android. Touch targets, bottom sheets, and thumb-friendly layouts take precedence.
4. **Status is sacred** — KYC status, payment status, application status are the core of this product. Make state changes visible, immediate, and unambiguous.
5. **Reduce friction, not features** — the app is feature-rich by necessity. The job is to make that complexity feel manageable, not to strip it out.

## Token Reference

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--primary` | `hsl(203 89% 45%)` | CTAs, links, focus rings |
| `--ios-blue` | `hsl(214 100% 59%)` | Interactive elements, highlights |
| `--accent` / `--ios-green` | `hsl(142 72% 44%)` | Success, completion, approvals |
| `--earth-warm` | `hsl(25 95% 53%)` | Warmth accents, warnings |
| `--destructive` | `hsl(0 84% 60%)` | Errors, rejections |
| `--ios-gray-light` | `hsl(220 14% 96%)` | Surfaces, card backgrounds |
| Font | Inter var | All text |
| Radius | `--radius` + `ios-card` (1rem) | Cards and modals |

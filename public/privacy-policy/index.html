Section 1

1. Introduction

BuildSynergy (Pty) Ltd ("BuildSynergy", "we", "us", or "our"), trading as MzanziHomes, operates the MzanziHomes mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our Service.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") of South Africa. By using the MzanziHomes app, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Officer: The MzanziHomes Information Officer is responsible for ensuring compliance with POPIA. You may contact our Information Officer at privacy@mzanzihomes.com.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the MzanziHomes application, including landlords, property managers, and tenants.

Section 2

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of the Service, and information from third-party services you connect to MzanziHomes.

2a. Account Information

When you create a MzanziHomes account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Profile photograph (optional)
  • Account type (landlord or tenant)
  • Password (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — never in plain text)

2b. Identity and KYC Documents

To comply with the Financial Intelligence Centre Act (FICA) and to verify user identity, we collect:

  • South African ID document or passport (photograph or scan)
  • Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement, or municipal account — dated within 3 months)
  • Selfie photograph for identity verification
  • Bank statements (for tenant income verification — shared only with landlords of properties you apply to)
  • Payslips or proof of income (optional, for rental applications)

KYC documents are encrypted at rest and in transit. They are shared with a specific landlord only upon your active application to their property, and only for the duration of the application process.

2c. Property Data

Landlords who list properties provide:

  • Property address and geolocation coordinates
  • Property photographs
  • Rental amount, deposit amount, and lease terms
  • Property description and features
  • Availability dates

2d. Location Data

With your permission, we collect your precise GPS location when you use property search, to show rentals near you. We do not track or store your location continuously — it is used only in real-time during an active search session and is not retained after the session ends. You may disable location access at any time in your device settings.

2e. Financial and Payment Data

MzanziHomes processes payments through Paystack, a PCI DSS-compliant payment processor. We collect:

  • Payment history (dates, amounts, status)
  • Landlord bank account details for rental payouts (stored securely by Paystack)

We do not store your card number, CVV, or full bank account number on our servers at any time. All sensitive payment credentials are handled exclusively by Paystack.

2f. Communications

In-app messages exchanged between landlords and tenants are stored on our servers. These messages may be reviewed by MzanziHomes support staff in the event of a dispute or reported violation of our Terms of Service.

2g. Device and Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • App version
  • Push notification token (for delivering notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • App usage patterns, screens viewed, and features used (via Firebase Analytics)
  • Performance and crash data (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • General usage metrics (via Vercel Analytics)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP (for analytics and fraud prevention)

Section 5

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to any third party. We share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • With landlords you apply to: Your profile and KYC documents are shared with the relevant landlord for evaluation purposes only.
  • With tenants who apply to your listing: Landlords can see applicant profiles, KYC documents, and application details.
  • With service providers: We share data with Supabase, Firebase, Paystack, Google Maps, and Vercel solely to operate the Service.
  • For legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of MzanziHomes users.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of MzanziHomes, your data may be transferred to the acquiring entity subject to equivalent privacy protections.
Section 6

6. Data Retention

  • Active account data — retained while your account is active
  • Account data after deletion — retained for 3 years after account closure to comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes
  • KYC documents — retained for 5 years from the date of the relevant transaction, as required by FICA
  • Payment records — retained for 7 years, as required by South African tax legislation (Income Tax Act)
  • Analytics data — aggregated and anonymised data may be retained indefinitely; identifiable data retained for 14 months by Firebase
  • Communications (messages) — retained for 3 years after the relevant tenancy ends

When retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

Section 7

7. Your Rights under POPIA

7a. Right of Access

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you. We will provide this within 30 days of a verified request.

7b. Right to Correction

You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information. Most account information can be updated directly within the MzanziHomes app.

7c. Right to Deletion

You may request that we delete your personal information. Some data must be retained for legal compliance (see Section 6) — we will inform you of anything we are unable to delete and explain why.

7d. Right to Object

You may object to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing purposes and opt out at any time.

7e. Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have violated your rights under POPIA, you may lodge a complaint with:

Information Regulator (South Africa)
Website: inforegulator.org.za
Email: complaints.IR@justice.gov.za
Phone: 012 406 4818

To exercise any of the above rights, contact us at privacy@mzanzihomes.com. We will verify your identity before processing any request.

Section 8

8. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Encryption at rest: All data stored in Supabase is encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Access controls: Row Level Security (RLS) is enforced at the database level — users can only access data they are authorised to see
  • Password security: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt via Supabase Auth — never stored in plain text
  • KYC document access: Identity documents are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket with expiring signed URLs — never publicly accessible
  • Payment security: All payment credentials are handled by Paystack (PCI DSS Level 1 compliant) — we never receive or store card details

Despite these measures, no system is completely secure. In the event of a data breach posing a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the Information Regulator in accordance with POPIA requirements.

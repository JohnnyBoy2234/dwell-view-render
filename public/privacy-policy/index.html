Section 3

3. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

3a. Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Role on the platform (landlord, property manager, or tenant)
  • Profile photograph (optional)

3b. Identity and Verification Documents

3c. Property and Tenancy Information

  • Property address and geolocation coordinates

  • Rental amount, deposit amount, and lease terms
  • Property description and features

3d. Payment and Financial Information

  • Transaction amounts, dates, and status
  • Payment references

We do not store card numbers, CVV codes, or full bank account numbers on our servers. All sensitive payment credentials are handled by our PCI DSS-compliant payment processor.

3e. Communications and Support Records

  • In-app messages between landlords and tenants
  • Support tickets and dispute records
  • Correspondence with our team

In-app messages may be reviewed by MzanziHomes support staff in the event of a reported dispute or Terms of Service violation.

3f. Location Information

With your permission, we collect your approximate or precise location when you use property search features. Precise GPS location is used only in real time during an active search session and is not stored after the session ends. You may disable location access at any time in your device settings.

3g. Device and Usage Data

  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • App version
  • Push notification token (for delivering notifications)
  • App usage patterns, screens viewed, and features used
  • Performance and crash data
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP (for analytics and fraud prevention)

Section 7

7. POPIA Grounds for Processing

Depending on the context, we process personal information on the following grounds permitted by section 11 of POPIA:

  • Contract: Processing is necessary to conclude or perform a contract to which you are a party, such as creating your account, processing a rental application, administering a lease, or making or receiving a payment.
  • Legal obligation: Processing is required to comply with a legal obligation, such as identity verification under FICA, financial record-keeping under tax legislation, or responding to a lawful demand from a regulatory authority or court.
  • Legitimate interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests or those of a third party — such as fraud prevention, security monitoring, service improvement, and dispute resolution — where those interests are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Consent: Where we rely on your consent — such as for optional features, certain marketing communications, or the collection of special personal information — we will request your consent separately, keep a record of it, and you may withdraw it at any time with future effect by contacting us at privacy@mzanzihomes.com. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

Section 12

12. Data Security

We take appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, damage, unauthorised destruction, unlawful access, and unlawful processing. These include:

  • Encryption in transit: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Encryption at rest: All data stored in Supabase is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Access controls: Row Level Security (RLS) is enforced at the database level — users can only access data they are authorised to see.
  • Password security: Passwords are hashed via Supabase Auth — never stored in plain text.
  • Document access: Identity documents are stored in a private Supabase Storage bucket with expiring signed URLs — never publicly accessible.
  • Payment security: All payment credentials are handled by Paystack (PCI DSS Level 1 compliant) — we never receive or store card details.
  • Ongoing review: We periodically review and update our security safeguards to address new or changed risks.
  • Operator oversight: All operators are required to maintain appropriate security safeguards and report incidents to us without delay.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We continuously work to improve our controls to minimise risk.

16. Special Personal Information

We do not intentionally require special personal information unless it is necessary and lawful for a specific purpose, such as establishing, exercising, or defending a legal right, or complying with a legal obligation. Where we process special personal information, we apply additional safeguards and do so only where POPIA permits.

If the scope of our identity verification or screening services expands in ways that may implicate special personal information, we will complete the required legal assessments before launch and update this policy accordingly.

Section 18

18. Your Rights under POPIA

Subject to POPIA and the Promotion of Access to Information Act ("PAIA"), you have the following rights:

18a. Right to Confirmation and Access

You may request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you, and access to a record or description of that information — including the categories of recipients to whom it has been disclosed. Confirmation of whether we hold your information is free of charge. Access to a record or description may be subject to a prescribed fee where permitted by law.

18b. Right to Correction or Deletion

You may request that we correct, update, or delete personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading, or unlawfully obtained, or that we are no longer authorised to retain. Most account information can be updated directly within the app. Where deletion is limited by a legal retention obligation, we will inform you of what we cannot delete and explain why.

18c. Right to Object

You may object on reasonable grounds to the processing of your personal information. You have an unconditional right to object to processing for the purpose of direct marketing at any time, free of charge. For other processing activities, we will consider your objection and cease or restrict processing unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

18d. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we rely on your consent as the basis for processing, you may withdraw that consent at any time with future effect. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

18e. Right to Information Quality

We take reasonable steps to ensure that the personal information we hold is complete, accurate, not misleading, and updated where necessary. We ask that you keep your account information current and notify us of any material changes.

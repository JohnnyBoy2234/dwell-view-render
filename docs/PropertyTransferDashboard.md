# Property Transfer Dashboard System

A comprehensive South African property sale dashboard system for managing private property transfers without estate agents.

## 🚀 Features

### 🟢 Seller Dashboard
- **Property Overview**: Address, sale price, buyer details, conveyancer info
- **Progress Tracker**: Visual progress through legal stages
- **Task Management**: Dynamic checklist with status tracking
- **Financial Summary**: Sale price, costs, net proceeds calculation
- **Communication**: Secure messaging with buyer and conveyancer

### 🔵 Buyer Dashboard  
- **Purchase Overview**: Property details, offer amount, seller info
- **Task List**: Finance, legal, and payment requirements
- **Payment Tracking**: Deposit, transfer duty, fees management
- **Document Management**: Upload and track required documents
- **Status Updates**: Real-time progress notifications

### ⚖️ Legal Progress Tracker
- **Stage Tracking**: Complete legal process visibility
- **Timeline Management**: Expected vs actual completion dates
- **Responsibility Assignment**: Clear party accountability
- **Status Indicators**: Color-coded progress (Grey/Blue/Green/Red)
- **Document Verification**: Attorney-verified document vault

### 📂 Document Management
- **Secure Vault**: Organized document storage
- **Categories**: Contracts, FICA, Compliance, Financial documents
- **Verification**: Attorney verification status
- **Access Control**: Role-based document access
- **Audit Trail**: Complete activity tracking

### 🔔 Notification System
- **Real-time Alerts**: Email and in-app notifications
- **Smart Triggers**: Status changes, payment due dates
- **Communication**: Integrated messaging system
- **Reminders**: Automated deadline notifications

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Access the Dashboard
1. Navigate to `/property-transfer`
2. Select your role (Seller/Buyer/Conveyancer)
3. View your personalized dashboard
4. Track progress and manage tasks

### Key Workflows

#### Seller Workflow
1. **Property Setup**: Add property details and sale information
2. **Document Upload**: Upload required documents (ID, compliance certificates)
3. **Task Management**: Complete seller-specific tasks
4. **Communication**: Respond to buyer and conveyancer messages
5. **Financial Tracking**: Monitor costs and net proceeds

#### Buyer Workflow
1. **Offer Management**: View and accept property offers
2. **Finance Setup**: Apply for bond or upload proof of funds
3. **Document Upload**: Submit FICA and supporting documents
4. **Payment Management**: Pay deposit, transfer duty, and fees
5. **Progress Tracking**: Monitor transfer progress

#### Conveyancer Workflow
1. **Case Management**: View all assigned transfers
2. **Document Review**: Verify uploaded documents
3. **Progress Updates**: Update legal process stages
4. **Communication**: Message buyers and sellers
5. **Finalization**: Complete registration and fund release

## 📊 Process Stages

### South African Property Transfer Process
1. **Negotiation** → Offer acceptance and agreement
2. **Bond Approval** → Buyer secures financing (if applicable)
3. **Compliance** → Seller completes legal requirements
4. **Lodgement** → Documents submitted to Deeds Office
5. **Registration** → Property officially transferred
6. **Completion** → Funds released to seller

### Timeline Estimates
- **Total Process**: 8-12 weeks typical
- **Bond Approval**: 2-4 weeks
- **Compliance**: 1-2 weeks
- **Lodgement**: 1 week
- **Registration**: 2-3 weeks
